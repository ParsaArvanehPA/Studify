⚡ Functionalist Approaches to Interpreting · EXAM CRAM

Christiane Nord, Ch. 6 | Just the essentials

🎯 Big Picture

Skopostheorie is a GENERAL theory → applies to interpreting too. Kade: "Translation" (capital T) = both Übersetzen (written) + Dolmetschen (oral).

📌 Key Difference: Correctability

Translation: can be corrected after writing
Interpreting: complete at moment of production; source presented once, texts transitory; same situational conditions

📌 Key Similarities

Both: seek Skopos, offer info about offer of info, need intratextual coherence, should be coherent with ST (fidelity rule)

📌 Training Reform: "From Interpreting to Translation"

Vermeer & Ammann (1990): start with "making someone understand" (interpreting in the original sense) → then written translation. Avoids word-for-word reproduction. Trains focus on communicative purpose.

📌 Pöchhacker: Simultaneous Interpreting

SI: TT produced in synchrony with ST production
Conference = hypertext: individual speeches aren't independent units — they're components of a greater whole. Skopos = at the CONFERENCE level, not individual text level.
Diaculture (Vermeer): When English = lingua franca, "culture" = shared professional background, not national. Transcends language borders.
