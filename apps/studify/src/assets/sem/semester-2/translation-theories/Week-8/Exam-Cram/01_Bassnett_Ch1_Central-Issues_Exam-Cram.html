⚡ Central Issues in Translation Studies · EXAM CRAM

Susan Bassnett, Ch. 1 | Just the essentials

🎯 Core

Translation belongs to SEMIOTICS, not just linguistics. "Language is the heart within the body of culture" — never separate the two.

📌 Equivalence

Popovic's 4 types: Linguistic (word) / Paradigmatic (grammar) / Stylistic (functional) / Textual (form + shape)
Invariant core: Stable semantic elements shared by ALL translations of a single text. Variants change form but not core. Equivalence ≠ sameness.

📌 Untranslatability

Catford: Linguistic (no TL substitute) vs. Cultural (situation absent from TL). BUT Bassnett: home, democracy show cultural untranslatability is in EVERY translation.
Mounin: "Never completely finished, never wholly impossible."

📌 Loss and Gain

Sameness impossible → translation involves BOTH loss AND gain. Translators can enrich or clarify the source. What's "lost" in one culture may be replaced in another.

📌 Visibility

Venuti (1995): Translators INVISIBLE in Anglo-American culture. Advocates foreignization over domestication as a more ethical stance. Lefevere: Translation = rewriting.

📌 Paz

"Every text is unique and, at the same time, the translation of another text."
