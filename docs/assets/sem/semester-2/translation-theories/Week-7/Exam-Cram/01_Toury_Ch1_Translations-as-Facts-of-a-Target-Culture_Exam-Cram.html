⚡ Translations as Facts of a Target Culture · EXAM CRAM

Gideon Toury, Ch. 1 | Just the essentials

🎯 The Axiom

Translations are facts of the TARGET culture. Their position, function, and form = determined by target culture considerations, not source.

📌 Toury vs. Skopostheorie

Both target-oriented but different goals: Toury = descriptive-explanatory (generalize about translational behaviour). Skopos = applied (improve practice).

📌 "Assumed Translation" — 3 Postulates

Source-Text Postulate: Assumption a prior text existed (even if you can't find it — enables study of pseudotranslations)
Transfer Postulate: Features were transferred from it
Relationship Postulate: Tangible relationships tie the TT to its assumed original

📌 Method: Discovery vs. Justification

Start with OBSERVABLES (translations). Map target segments → assumed source segments → coupled pairs → establish equivalence as the norm for that pair. The progression is helical, not linear.

📌 Equivalence (Redefined)

NOT prescriptive. Equivalence is taken as a GIVEN — study the WEB OF RELATIONS. It = the translation relationship that constitutes the NORM for the pair.
