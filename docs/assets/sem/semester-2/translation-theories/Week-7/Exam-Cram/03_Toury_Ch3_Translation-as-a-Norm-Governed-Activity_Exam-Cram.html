⚡ Translation as a Norm-Governed Activity · EXAM CRAM

Gideon Toury, Ch. 3 | Just the essentials

🎯 Big Picture

Translation = culturally determined, norm-governed. Norms mediate between abstract competence + actual performance. They're RECONSTRUCTED from regularities.

📌 Norm vs. Convention

Convention: Vague, non-binding agreement (from "sociability")
Norm: Translates values into performance instructions. Involves SANCTIONS (positive/negative). Specific enough to guide behaviour.

📌 The Continuum

RULES (objective) — NORMS (intersubjective, central) — IDIOSYNCRASIES (subjective). Borderlines are diffuse; norms shift over time.

📌 Adequacy vs. Acceptability

Adequacy = adherence to SOURCE norms. Acceptability = adherence to TARGET norms.
Every translation = a BLEND/COMPROMISE. Never 100% either. Incompatibility between the two = normal state.

📌 Competing Norms (3 Types)

Central/mainstream (trendy) — Remnants of previous mainstream (dated) — Rudiments of new set (avant-garde)
Novices = safe, dated norms. Experienced/prestigious translators = drive change.
