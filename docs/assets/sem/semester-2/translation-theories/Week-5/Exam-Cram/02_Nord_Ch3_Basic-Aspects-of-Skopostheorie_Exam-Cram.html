🎯 The Three Rules (Know the Hierarchy)

SKOPOS RULE > Coherence Rule (intratextual) > Fidelity Rule (intertextual)
Skopos rule: Purpose governs everything. "The end justifies the means." Free or faithful? Depends on the purpose.
Coherence rule: The TT must make sense in the receiver's situation — "part of" it.
Fidelity rule: Relationship between TT and ST exists, but its form depends on the Skopos.

📌 Translation Brief

Client specifies WHAT purpose (addressees, time, place, medium, function). NOT HOW to translate — that's the translator's expert job. Often the client doesn't give one — experienced translators infer the Skopos from the situation.

📌 Culture

Culture (Vermeer): norms + conventions you must know to behave acceptably (or deviate). Culturemes: phenomena specific to one culture when compared to another. Translating = comparing cultures; no neutral standpoint.

📌 Adequacy vs. Equivalence

Adequacy: dynamic, process-oriented — "adequate to" the brief. The SUPREME concept.
Equivalence: static, result-oriented — equal value between texts. In Skopostheorie = functional equivalence only.
