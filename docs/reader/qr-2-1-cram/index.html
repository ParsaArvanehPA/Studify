Session 2.1: Ayat al-Kursi (Verses 255-257)
Quick Review | The Greatest Verse
⏱️ EXAM TIP: Focus on the 9 divine attributes in v.255, the meaning of "no compulsion" in v.256, and light vs. darkness symbolism in v.257!

📋 Fast Facts

Verse Name:

Ayat al-Kursi (آية الكرسي) = "The Throne Verse"

Location:

Surah Al-Baqarah, Verse 255

Status:

Prophet ﷺ said: "The GREATEST verse in the Book of Allah" (Sahih Muslim)

Verses Covered:

255 (Ayat al-Kursi) + 256 (No Compulsion) + 257 (Light vs. Darkness)

📖 Verse 255 - Ayat al-Kursi (VERY TESTABLE!)

⚠️ EXAM WARNING: The 9 divine attributes are frequently tested! Memorize them in order!

🔢 The 9 Divine Attributes - MEMORIZE!

#AttributeArabicMeaning
1Absolute Onenessلا إِلهَ إِلاَّ هُوَNo deity except Him
2The Ever-LivingالْحَيُّAl-Hayy - Eternal life

🔥 Ism al-A'zam:

Al-Hayy Al-Qayyum = Greatest Name of Allah (according to many scholars)

🔑 Key Terms

كُرْسِيّ (Kursi):

Throne/Footstool - Ibn Abbas: "Kursi is Allah's footstool, the 'Arsh (Throne) is even greater!"

سِنَةٌ (Sinah):

Drowsiness/slumber - Allah never even drowsy, totally alert 24/7

📚 Prophetic Traditions (Know These!)

  • Before Sleep: Recite for protection - "Satan won't approach till morning" (Bukhari)
  • After Each Prayer: "Nothing prevents entering Paradise except death" (An-Nasa'i)
  • Protection: Guards home & possessions

📖 Verse 256 - No Compulsion in Religion

لا إِكْراهَ فِي الدِّينِ قَدْ تَبَيَّنَ الرُّشْدُ مِنَ الْغَيِّ...

V.256Translation:

"No compulsion in religion. Right path clear from wrong."

🎯 Core Principle - TESTABLE!

لا إِكْراهَ فِي الدِّينِ:

NO compulsion/force in accepting religion - revolutionary principle of religious freedom!

Why No Compulsion? (Common Exam Question)

ReasonExplanation
Truth is Clear"Right path clear from wrong" - when truth manifest, force unnecessary
Faith Requires WillForced conversion = hypocrisy, not genuine faith
Divine DesignAllah created humans with free will - compulsion violates this
Test of CharacterChoice between belief/disbelief is THE fundamental human test

🔑 Key Terms

الطَّاغُوتِ (Taghut):

False deities/tyrants/anything worshipped besides Allah - includes idols, tyrannical powers, Satan, superstitions

الْعُرْوَةِ الْوُثْقى (Al-'Urwatul-Wuthqa):

"Trustworthy handhold" - metaphor for faith in Allah; لا انْفِصامَ لَها = NO BREAK in it (unbreakable!)

📚 Historical Context

Occasion of Revelation:

Ansari mothers who'd vowed to raise children as Jews wanted to force conversion to Islam - this verse PROHIBITED it

📖 Verse 257 - Light vs. Darkness (VERY SYMBOLIC!)

اللَّـهُ وَلِيُّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا يُخْرِجُهُمْ مِنَ الظُّلُماتِ إِلَى النُّورِ...

V.257

Allah brings believers from DARKNESS → LIGHT; Taghut brings disbelievers from LIGHT → DARKNESSES

🌓 The Cosmic Duality - TESTABLE!

ElementBelieversDisbelievers
GuardianAllahTaghut (false powers)
DirectionDarkness → LightLight → Darkness
Numberالنُّورِ (SINGULAR)الظُّلُماتِ (PLURAL)
Destination(Paradise)Eternal Fire
🔥 CRITICAL:

Light = SINGULAR (truth is ONE); Darkness = PLURAL (error is MANY paths)

💡 What Do Light & Darkness Symbolize?

Light (النور)Darkness (الظُّلُمات)
Divine guidanceIgnorance & confusion
Knowledge & wisdomSpiritual blindness
Clarity of purposeAnxiety & despair
Truth & realityFalsehood & illusion
Tragic Reversal:

Disbelievers go FROM light INTO darkness - they HAD access to truth but REJECTED it!

🎯 Cheat Sheet - Must Memorize

📝 Quick Reference

Verse 255 (Ayat al-Kursi)

  • ✅ 9 divine attributes (see table above)
  • ✅ Al-Hayy Al-Qayyum = Ism al-A'zam
  • ✅ Kursi = Footstool (smaller than 'Arsh)
  • ✅ No sleep, no drowsiness - constant vigilance
  • ✅ Complete knowledge of past/future

Verse 256 (No Compulsion)

  • ✅ لا إِكْراهَ فِي الدِّينِ = No force in religion
  • ✅ Truth is self-evident - force unnecessary
  • ✅ Taghut = false deities/tyrants
  • ✅ Al-'Urwatul-Wuthqa = unbreakable handhold of faith

Verse 257 (Light vs. Darkness)

  • ✅ Allah = guardian of believers → Light
  • ✅ Taghut = allies of disbelievers → Darkness
  • ✅ Light SINGULAR, Darkness PLURAL
  • ✅ Truth is ONE, error is MANY

🎓 Exam Strategy Tips

✏️ LIKELY EXAM QUESTIONS:
  1. What is Ayat al-Kursi? → The greatest verse in Quran (v.255)
  2. Name 3 divine attributes from v.255 → Al-Hayy, Al-Qayyum, Complete Knowledge (any 3 from the 9)
  3. What is Ism al-A'zam? → Al-Hayy Al-Qayyum (Greatest Name)
  4. Kursi vs. 'Arsh? → Kursi = Footstool (smaller), 'Arsh = Throne (greater)
  5. Meaning of "no compulsion in religion"? → Cannot force someone to accept Islam - faith requires free will
  6. What is Taghut? → False deities, tyrants, anything worshipped besides Allah
  7. Light singular, darkness plural - why? → Truth is ONE path, error has MANY paths
  8. Prophetic hadith about Ayat al-Kursi? → Recite before sleep for protection, after prayers for Paradise
