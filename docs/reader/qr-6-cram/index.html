Session 6: Surah Al-Buruj (1-22) | Quick Review
⏱️ EXAM TIP: Focus on the three oaths, People of the Trench story, six divine attributes, and the Preserved Tablet!

📋 Fast Facts

Al-Buruj (البروج) = "The Constellations"

Type:

85 |

22

Theme:

Divine oaths → Martyrs burned in trenches → Warning to persecutors → Quran preserved

📖 Verses 1-3 - Three Divine Oaths

OathArabicMeaning
1. Sky with Constellationsالسَّماءِ ذاتِ الْبُرُوجِCosmic order, divine design
2. Promised Dayالْيَوْمِ الْمَوْعُودِDay of Judgment
3. Witness & Witnessedشاهِدٍ وَ مَشْهُودٍAngels witnessing, Friday/Arafah, creation
البُرُوج (Al-Buruj):

"Towers/fortresses in sky" - zodiac constellations. Symbol of cosmic order.

📖 Verses 4-9 - People of the Trench (VERY TESTABLE!)

قُتِلَ أَصْحابُ الْأُخْدُودِ: "Cursed were the makers of the trench"

🔥 What Happened?

  • Tyrannical king (possibly Najran, Yemen ~520 CE)
  • Dug trenches filled with fire and fuel (النَّارِ ذاتِ الْوَقُودِ)
  • Ultimatum: Renounce faith OR burn
  • Believers chose martyrdom - thousands burned alive
  • Persecutors sat watching (قُعُودٌ... شُهُودٌ)

💔 Their Only "Crime"

وَ ما نَقَمُوا مِنْهُمْ إِلاَّ أَنْ يُؤْمِنُوا بِاللَّـهِ:

"They resented them ONLY for believing in Allah"

→ No other crime! Pure religious persecution. Exposes total injustice.

👶 Famous Story

Young boy encouraged mother to jump into fire - temporary pain vs. eternal hellfire. Baby miraculously spoke!

📖 Verses 10-11 - Warning & Promise

PersecutorsBelievers
Tortured believers (فَتَنُوا)Believed & did good
Did not repent (لَمْ يَتُوبُوا)Remained steadfast
Punishment of Hell (عَذابُ جَهَنَّمَ)Gardens with rivers (جَنَّاتٌ)
Punishment of Burning (عَذابُ الْحَرِيقِ)Great Attainment (الْفَوْزُ الْكَبِيرُ)
KEY:

Even heinous criminals CAN repent (ثُمَّ لَمْ يَتُوبُوا) - door of mercy always open!

📖 Verses 12-16 - Six Divine Attributes (MEMORIZE!)

AttributeArabicMeaning
1. Severe Vengeanceبَطْشَ... لَشَدِيدٌPunishment is severe
2. Originator & Repeaterيُبْدِئُ وَ يُعِيدُCreates & resurrects
3. The ForgivingالْغَفُورُForgives sins
4. The AffectionateالْوَدُودُFull of love
5. Owner of Throneذُو الْعَرْشِ الْمَجِيدُSupreme authority
6. Effecter of Willفَعَّالٌ لِما يُرِيدُDoes what He wills
الْوَدُودُ (Al-Wadūd):

One of Allah's Beautiful Names - "The Affectionate/Loving"

Balance:

Severe punishment (justice) + Deep love (mercy) - both in same passage!

📖 Verses 17-20 - Historical Examples

🎯 Two Destroyed Nations

NationTheir CrimeTheir End
Pharaoh (فِرْعَوْنَ)Persecuted believers, claimed divinityDrowned in Red Sea
Thamud (ثَمُودَ)Advanced civilization, rejected truthDestroyed by earthquake
MESSAGE:

Makers of trench = following path of destroyed tyrants!

🔍 Allah's Encompassing Knowledge

وَ اللَّـهُ مِنْ وَرائِهِمْ مُحِيطٌ:

"Allah is, from behind them, encompassing"

→ No matter how powerful, oppressors cannot escape divine justice!

📖 Verses 21-22 - The Preserved Quran

بَلْ هُوَ قُرْآنٌ مَجِيدٌ فِي لَوْحٍ مَحْفُوظٍ

"But this is an honored Quran, in a Preserved Tablet"

Two Key Terms:

1. قُرْآنٌ مَجِيدٌ:

Honored/Glorious Quran - elevated status

2. لَوْحٍ مَحْفُوظٍ:

Preserved Tablet (Al-Lawh al-Mahfūdh)

Significance:

  • ✅ Pre-existed with Allah before creation
  • ✅ Cannot be corrupted or changed
  • ✅ Eternal, permanent truth
  • ✅ 1400+ years unchanged = proof of divine protection

🎓 Likely Exam Questions

  1. What does Al-Buruj mean? → The Constellations (great stars/zodiac)
  2. Name the three oaths? → Sky with constellations, Promised Day, Witness & witnessed
  3. Who were Ashab al-Ukhdud? → People of the Trench (martyrs burned for faith)
  4. What was their only "crime"? → Believing in Allah (pure religious persecution)
  5. Where/when did this happen? → Possibly Najran, Yemen ~520 CE
  6. Can persecutors still repent? → YES - door of mercy always open (ثُمَّ لَمْ يَتُوبُوا)
  7. Name 3 divine attributes from v.12-16? → Severe vengeance, Originator/Repeater, Forgiving, Affectionate, Owner of Throne, Effecter of Will
  8. What does Al-Wadūd mean? → The Affectionate/Loving (one of Allah's names)
  9. Two destroyed nations mentioned? → Pharaoh and Thamud
  10. What is Lawh Mahfudh? → Preserved Tablet where Quran is recorded
  11. What does "Allah encompasses them" mean? → Allah surrounds oppressors with knowledge/power - can't escape justice

⚡ Last-Minute Review

If You Remember NOTHING ELSE:

  • ✅ V.1-3: Three oaths (Constellations, Promised Day, Witness/Witnessed)
  • ✅ V.4-9: People of Trench - believers burned alive for faith, only "crime" = believing in Allah
  • ✅ V.10-11: Persecutors → Hell's burning (unless repent!), Believers → Gardens (great attainment)
  • ✅ V.12-16: Six attributes - Severe vengeance + Forgiving + Affectionate (Al-Wadūd) + Owner of Throne + more
  • ✅ V.17-20: Pharaoh & Thamud destroyed - Allah encompasses all oppressors
  • ✅ V.21-22: Quran is HONORED, preserved in LAWH MAHFUDH - cannot be corrupted!
  • ✅ Theme: Truth & believers triumph despite persecution - cosmic order, divine justice, eternal preservation
