Type:

120 verses

Main Topics (this session):

Divine tests, personal responsibility, testimony law, ultimate accountability

📖 Verse 94 - The Hunting Test

يا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا لَيَبْلُوَنَّكُمُ اللَّـهُ بِشَيْ‏ءٍ مِنَ الصَّيْدِ تَنالُهُ أَيْدِيكُمْ وَ رِماحُكُمْ...

V.94Key Message:

Allah will test you with game/hunting that your hands and spears can easily reach → To reveal who fears Him in the unseen

🎯 Context - TESTABLE!

Historical Setting:

Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (6 AH / 628 CE)

Situation:

Muslims in state of

ihram

(pilgrimage consecration) - hunting FORBIDDEN

The Test:

Abundant game everywhere, easy to catch, but must RESTRAIN despite hunger

Key Terms to Know:

Arabic Transliteration Meaning
لَيَبْلُوَنَّكُمُ La-yabluwannakum "Surely will test you"
بِالْغَيْبِ Bil-ghayb "In the unseen" / "when unseen"
تَنالُهُ أَيْدِيكُمْ وَ رِماحُكُمْ Tanāluhu aydīkum wa rimāhukum "Your hands and spears can reach"

💡 The Point of the Test

Purpose:

"So that Allah may make evident those who fear Him unseen"

What's Being Tested:

Not strength or skill, but RESTRAINT and God-consciousness (تقوى)

The Challenge:

Test is through something EASY to get, not impossible - restraint is hard!

🔥 Severe Warning

"Whoever transgresses after that - painful punishment"
  • After CLEAR warning = more severe
  • After knowing it's a TEST = no excuse
  • Deliberate sin > ignorant sin

📖 Verse 105 - Personal Responsibility

يا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا عَلَيْكُمْ أَنْفُسَكُمْ لا يَضُرُّكُمْ مَنْ ضَلَّ إِذَا اهْتَدَيْتُمْ...

V.105Translation:

"Upon you is [responsibility for] yourselves. Those who go astray will not harm you when you have been guided."

⚠️ COMMON MISINTERPRETATION (EXAM FAVORITE!)

WRONG Interpretation CORRECT Interpretation
❌ "Don't worry about others, just focus on yourself" ✅ "After you fulfill your duty to advise others, their misguidance won't harm YOU"
❌ Abandon commanding good/forbidding evil ✅ You MUST try to guide, but you're not responsible for their final choice

📖 Verses 106-108 - Testimony Law (VERY TESTABLE!)

⚠️ EXAM WARNING: Testimony procedures are frequently tested! Know the witness requirements and counter-testimony process!

🧮 Witness Requirements for Bequests

Situation Witness Requirement
Normal circumstances Two just men from among you (Muslims)
While traveling Two others from outside (may be NON-Muslims)
🔥 REVOLUTIONARY:

Rare permission to accept NON-MUSLIM testimony (when traveling and no Muslim witnesses available)

🔐 Oath Protocol - MEMORIZE

Procedural Safeguards (V.106)

  1. Timing: "After the prayer" (heightened spiritual awareness)
  2. Sacred Oath: "Swear by Allah"
  3. No Bribery: "We won't exchange it for a price"
  4. No Favoritism: "Even if he's a near relative"
  5. Divine Accountability: "We won't withhold Allah's testimony"

🔄 Counter-Testimony Process (V.107-108)

IF perjury discovered:
  1. Two others from rightful heirs can step forward
  2. They take counter-oath: "Our testimony is truer"
  3. They swear they haven't transgressed
  4. Their testimony SUPERSEDES the false one
Purpose (V.108):

"More likely they'll give testimony truthfully" - knowing testimony can be challenged = DETERRENT against lying

📚 Historical Case - Know This!

The Case of Tameem al-Dari

  • Situation: Muslim man (Budayl) traveling with 2 Christians, makes will, then dies
  • Problem: Silver vessel missing - Christian witnesses denied taking it
  • Discovery: Vessel found being sold in Mecca by those same witnesses!
  • Resolution: Muslim heirs gave counter-testimony, property returned
  • Lesson: Counter-testimony mechanism WORKS

📖 Verse 109 - Day of Judgment

يَوْمَ يَجْمَعُ اللَّـهُ الرُّسُلَ فَيَقُولُ ما ذا أُجِبْتُمْ قالُوا لا عِلْمَ لَنا إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ عَلاَّمُ الْغُيُوبِ

V.109Translation:

Day when Allah gathers messengers and asks "What response did you receive?" They say "We have no knowledge. You are Knower of the unseen."

🤔 Why Don't Prophets Answer? (Common Exam Question)

Reason Explanation
Overwhelming Awe Magnitude of Day overwhelms even messengers
Complete Knowledge Only Allah knows full truth - outward & inward
Epistemic Humility Acknowledging limits of human knowledge
Deference Defer to Allah's superior knowledge
Connection to Testimony Theme:

Human testimony can be false, but divine knowledge is ABSOLUTE and PERFECT - all hidden things revealed!

🎯 Cheat Sheet - Must Memorize

📝 Quick Reference

Verse 94 - Hunting Test

  • ✅ Context: Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, Muslims in ihram
  • ✅ Test: Game within easy reach but must restrain
  • ✅ Purpose: Reveal who fears Allah "unseen"
  • ✅ Warning: Painful punishment for transgression

Verse 105 - Personal Responsibility

  • ✅ NOT "ignore others" - MUST try to guide
  • ✅ After you advise, their choice ≠ your burden
  • ✅ Abu Bakr warned against misuse of this verse

Verses 106-108 - Testimony Law

  • ✅ Normal: 2 just Muslim men
  • ✅ Traveling: 2 from outside (may be non-Muslim)
  • ✅ Oath: After prayer, swear by Allah, no bribery/favoritism
  • ✅ Counter-testimony possible if perjury discovered

Verse 109 - Ultimate Testimony

  • ✅ Prophets defer to Allah's complete knowledge
  • ✅ All hidden things will be revealed
  • ✅ Divine knowledge > human testimony

Arabic Terms You MUST Know

1. لَيَبْلُوَنَّكُمُ

(La-yabluwannakum) = Surely will test you

2. بِالْغَيْبِ

(Bil-ghayb) = In/when unseen

(Istahaqqā ithman) = Were guilty of sin/perjury

('Allāmul-ghuyūb) = Knower of all unseen
