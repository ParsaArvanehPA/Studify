Session 3: Surah Al-Insan (1-22) | Quick Review
⏱️ EXAM TIP: Focus on the binary choice (grateful vs ungrateful), characteristics of the righteous, and Paradise descriptions!

📋 Fast Facts

Name:

Al-Insan (الإنسان) = "The Human" | Ad-Dahr (الدهر) = "Time"

Type:

Medinan |

Chapter:

76 |

Verses Studied:

1-22

Theme:

Human origin → free will → righteous deeds → Paradise reward

📖 Verses 1-3 - Human Origin & Choice

V.1:

"Was there not a time when man was nothing worth mentioning?"

نُطْفَةٍ أَمْشاجٍ (Nutfatin Amshāj):

Mixed sperm-drop (genetic material from both parents) - SCIENTIFIC ACCURACY!

THE BINARY CHOICE:

إِمَّا شاكِراً وَ إِمَّا كَفُوراً = Either GRATEFUL or UNGRATEFUL

📖 Verses 7-10 - Character of the Righteous (VERY TESTABLE!)

🎯 Three Characteristics

TraitDescription
1. Fulfill vowsيُوفُونَ بِالنَّذْرِ - Keep promises
2. Fear Judgment Dayيَخافُونَ يَوْماً... - Day عَبُوساً قَمْطَرِيراً (austere/distressful)
3. Feed othersGive food despite loving it themselves

🍞 Who Do They Feed?

  1. مِسْكِين (Miskīn): The needy/poor
  2. يَتِيم (Yatīm): The orphan
  3. أَسِير (Asīr): The captive/prisoner

💎 Their Motivation

"We feed you only for Allah's countenance (لِوَجْهِ اللَّـهِ). We want NO reward or thanks from you!"
Historical Context: Many say revealed about Prophet's family giving their food to needy for 3 nights, breaking fast with only water!

📖 Verses 11-22 - Paradise Rewards

🏆 8 Key Rewards

RewardDetails
ProtectionFrom evil of Judgment Day
Radiance & joyنَضْرَة (brightness) وَ سُرُور (happiness)
Garden & silkجَنَّة وَ حَرِير - for their patience
ThronesReclining comfortably, no sun/cold
Shade & fruitsNear shade, easy-to-reach fruits
Silver vesselsServed in silver & crystal
Ginger drinkFrom spring زَنْجَبِيل (Zanjabil)
Eternal servantsLike scattered pearls

🔑 Key Vocabulary - MEMORIZE!

Must-Know Terms

1. نُطْفَةٍ أَمْشاجٍ:

Mixed sperm-drop

2. شاكِر / كَفُور:

Grateful / Ungrateful

3. لِوَجْهِ اللَّـهِ:

For Allah's face/countenance (pure sincerity)

4. عَبُوساً قَمْطَرِيراً:

Austere and distressful (Judgment Day)

5. كافُور:

Camphor (cool, fragrant Paradise drink)

6. زَنْجَبِيل:

Ginger

7. سَلْسَبِيل:

Name of Paradise spring (smooth-flowing)

🎓 Likely Exam Questions

  1. What is the binary choice in v.3? → Grateful (شاكِر) or Ungrateful (كَفُور)
  2. What does "Nutfatin Amshāj" mean? → Mixed sperm-drop (both parents' genetic material)
  3. 3 groups they feed? → Needy, orphan, captive
  4. Their motivation for charity? → لِوَجْهِ اللَّـهِ (purely for Allah), no reward expected
  5. Describe Judgment Day (v.10)? → عَبُوساً قَمْطَرِيراً (austere/distressful)
  6. Name 2 Paradise drinks? → Kafur (camphor), Zanjabil (ginger)
  7. What is Salsabil? → Spring in Paradise (smooth-flowing)

⚡ Last-Minute Review

If You Remember NOTHING ELSE:

  • ✅ V.1-3: Human from nothing → Choice: Grateful OR Ungrateful
  • ✅ V.7-10: Righteous = fulfill vows, fear Judgment, feed needy/orphan/captive FOR ALLAH ALONE
  • ✅ V.11-22: Paradise rewards = protection, joy, garden, silk, drinks (Kafur, Zanjabil, Salsabil)
  • ✅ Key: لِوَجْهِ اللَّـهِ = Pure sincerity, expect nothing from people
  • ✅ Scientific: نُطْفَةٍ أَمْشاجٍ = Mixed genetic material (1400 years ago!)
