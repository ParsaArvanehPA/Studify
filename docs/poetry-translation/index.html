Session 1 · 6 coupletsThe guile of eyes
جلسه اولفریب چشم
Couplet 1بیت ۱

فریب شیوه‌ی چشم تو آشنای من است

The guile in your eyes, well I know, Familiar to me, their deception.

نسیم گیسوی جعدت گره گشای من است

The breeze from your hair, in tangles tossed, Eases my woes in its way.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

رهی که پای نهادی حریم خلوت ماست

Where your foot has fallen, my retreat does rest, In this place, has been pressed. Your way, a haven for my solitude's need, where years, my epochs walked in thought and in deed.

که سالهاست همین خطه جای پای من است

..where years, my epochs walked in thought and in deed

Couplet 3بیت ۳

ز آتشی که به پا کرده‌ای ندانستی

You're unaware of the inferno set in wake,

نوای هر نی دل سوخته ز نای من است

Every sad reed's tune, my flute does make. Your spark, the cause of this sad sound, In my flute's lament, its source is found.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

امیر ملک وجود و انیس و مونس من

Amir Malek, You, my joy, my wealth, my heart's repose,

به هر کجا که تو باشی همان سرای من است

Where You do reside, my spirit goes. In Your company, solace I do find, Your locale, my abode, in heart and mind.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

نگاه کردم و دیدم که دل به سینه‌ی تو

I gazed and saw, within your breast so true,

چنان دل من سر گشته مبتلای من است

A heart like mine, with afflictions not few. Bewildered as my own, your heart does bear, The same burden of woe in our shared care.

Couplet 6بیت ۶

همیشه بی تو دل من بهانه میگیرد

Ever without you, my heart does find pleas,

بهانه‌ای کن و باز که این دوای من است

Invent an excuse, return, bring me ease. In your absence, my soul does reasons seek, Your return, the remedy that I bespeak.

Session 2 · 7 coupletsSweet Lip's Silence
جلسه دومسکوت لب شیرین
Couplet 1بیت ۱

کسی ز حال دل من خبر نمیگیرد

No soul inquires of my heart's weary state,

ز خاطری که دگر شعر تر نمیگیرد

From a mind where once poetry did create, Now silent, dry, its verses no longer flow, Lost in a world where no one seeks to know.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

چنانکه دلبری آموختی وفا آموز

As coquetry you've mastered, now teach fidelity,

که جز وفای تو این وصل سر نمیگیرد

For union's form needs faith's sincerity. In this bond, your loyalty must take its shape, Lest love's design should falter and escape.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

دلم هوای سفر دارد ای دریغا دل

My heart yearns to journey, oh, pity its plight,

که بی حضور تو شوق سفر نمیگیرد

That finds no joy in travel without your light. For in your presence alone, it finds its grace, Without you, every journey loses its embrace.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

بیان درد جگرسوزت ای فرشته جان

O Angel of Life, in these lines I impart,

یقین بدان که در این مختصر نمیگیرد

The tale of my heart's consuming, fiery dart. Yet know this truth, in brevity's verse confined, Its depth of pain, in full, can never be enshrined.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

دوای درد من این شعر های رنگین نیست

These lively verses, though fair, mend not my pain,

بیا که مرغ دلم بی تو پر نمیگیرد

Come, for without you, my heart's bird is in chain. Its wings are stilled, in your absence, they rest, Only in your proximity, can it fly its best.

Couplet 6بیت ۶

تمام خاطره ها با تو رنگ می‌گیرد

With you, each memory takes on a vivid hue,

چنان که عشق از این خوب تر نمیگیرد

For love, in its finest form, rings ever true.

Couplet 7بیت ۷

سکوت هر لب شیرین صدای وسوسه‌ای

In every sweet lip's silence, temptation does dwell,

ترانه ای است که در هر هنر نمیگیرد

A song beyond art's reach, in its charm's spell. Uncaught in canvas or verse, its melody soars free, A siren's call, in silent charm, a mystery to see.

Session 2 · 7 coupletsStory of love
جلسه دومقصه عشق
Couplet 1بیت ۱

می‌کُشد عشقِ جگر سوز تو آخر ما را

Your searing love, in time, shall be our end,

عاشقِ سوخته‌ی شیفته‌ی شیدا را

A lover burnt, in infatuation's bend. Consumed by passion, frenzied and forlorn, In the blaze of your love, we're torn and worn.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

به خیالِ تو قسم بی‌تو دلم می‌گیرد

In thoughts of you, my heart does sorely pine,

بی‌خیالِ تو، نخواهم همه دنیا را

Without your memory, the world loses its shine. Each heartbeat echoes with a deep yearn, For lacking your memory, the world I spurn.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

آمدی خانه‌ی تنهاییِ من ویران شد

You came, and lo! My lone abode did fall,

تا که آباد کنی با قدمت فردا را

Your step did herald a brighter morrow's call. Your coming made the very ground to quake, A future lit by you, a tale to make.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

گاه لبخند تماشای صفِ مروارید

At times your smile, like pearls in perfect row,

بی‌گمان باز کند دیده‌ی نابینا را

Bright and clear, to the sightless eyes does show. Undoubtedly, it lights the way, the dark to bind, With such radiance, even the blind's eyes find.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

چشم‌هایی که زلالند چنان آب بهشت

Eyes as lucid as paradise's waters serene,

موجِ زلفی که به یغما ببرد دریا را

Amidst a wave of hair, like a sea, unseen. Their clarity rivals the purest ocean deep, In their gaze, secrets of celestial waters keep.

Couplet 6بیت ۶

این صدای سخنِ توست که در شعرِ من است

This is the sound of your voice in my verse,

یاد هایی است چنین ماهرویِ زیبا را

Memories of such a moon-faced beauty, in my song I rehearse.

Couplet 7بیت ۷

قصه‌ی عشقِ تو پایان‌نپذیرد هیهات

The tale of your love, endless, brings such woe,

همچو مجنون بکشم بارِ غمِ لیلا را

Like Majnun, Layla's grief upon me does bestow. In this unceasing tale, heavy it sits, Bearing the burden of a love that never quits.

Session 3 · 6 coupletsSarah and the flood
جلسه سومسارا و سیل
Couplet 1بیت ۱

سارای مرا سیل نبرده‌است به جایی

The deluge has not born my Sarah away,

خورشید نگیرد به سر انگشت بلایی

Nor does the sun in disaster's fingers sway. In flood, she remains untouched, unharmed, And in calamity, the sun's grace is not alarmed.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

آمد که کند خاک مرا لایق افلاک

It came to lift my dust to heaven's height,

هم رفت که افلاک شود رنگ خدایی

And left, that heaven might bear God's light. To render my earthly form divine in worth, Then departed, coloring the heavens with mirth.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

ما دلشدگان جان به ره دوست فشانیم

We, the forlorn in love, our lives have lain, On the beloved's path amidst joy and pain.

سودای وفا نه که به امید جفایی

Not with the hope of true faithfulness to find, But in the anticipation of betrayal unkind.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

پیوسته به گوش است مرا پند زمانه

By time's stern teachings, I am ever chided,

شاهان ننشینند مقابل به گدایی

That kings to beggars are not seated, sided. In life's harsh course, this truth does clearly appear, High thrones and lowly grounds do not draw near.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

لبریز وصالم تهی از غربت هجران

Full am i with union, void of parting's grief,

چون من همه اویم چه غم از داغ جدایی

For wholly hers I am, in love's firm belief. Of separation's sorrows, I take no heed, As in her hold, my heart has all it does need.

Couplet 6بیت ۶

سر تا سر هستی همه آیینه‌ی رویت

From start to finish, life does your visage reflect,

هیهات ز دام تو تمنّای رهایی

Alas, from your snare, no release to expect. In every corner, your countenance see, From your entrapment, no hope to flee.

Session 3 · 9 coupletsLions in Foxes' Grasp
جلسه سومشیرها در دست روباهان اسیر
Couplet 1بیت ۱

دوست دارم بازوی حیدر شوم

I yearn to be as Haydar's arm, strong and sure,

مردم آشفته را یاور شوم

To help the troubled souls, their burdens to endure. With strength and might, like Haydar in his grace, To offer aid, and solace in life's race.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

دوست دارم چون کبوترهای مست

I long to soar like pigeons, inebriated in flight,

پرکشم زین عالم بیداد و پست

Far from this world unjust, lowly, escaping to the light. On wings unburdened, towards the skies so grand, Away from earthly toils, to a higher, freer land.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

چون نسیمی بگذرم از این جهان

As a breeze, through this world I'd lightly roam,

رسم نامردی براندازم عیان

Boldly overturning customs that dishonor's home. With the wind's gentle might, yet firm in my quest, Challenging wrongful norms in pursuit of what's best.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

پرده‌ی ابهام هستی برکشم

I'd reveal the enigma of existence, clear and true,

تا مگر خامُش شود این آتشم

In hopes this inner fire might be quenched anew. Lifting life's obscure veil to see its core, Perhaps to calm the flames that in me roar.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

راز خود با رازدان نجوا کنم

I'd share my hidden truths with those who know,

تا کند از سرّ هستی آگهم

To grasp being's essence, in wisdom's glow. In whispered confidences with the understanding few, Seeking clarity, life's mysteries to construe.

Couplet 6بیت ۶

از میان آیه‌های گونه‌گون

Amidst the tapestry of varied verse,

رمز بگشایم ز مالاتعلمون

I'd seek to unravel the unknown, diverse. Exploring the depths of 'what you know not, To uncover secrets in the poetic knot.

Couplet 7بیت ۷

وای ازین غربت سرای دلفریب

Alas, for this land of estrangement, so deceiving,

زاغکی اندر نمای عندلیب

A crow dons the bulbul's guise, misleading, grieving. In this realm of illusion, where truths are concealed, False fronts are sung, their true nature unsealed.

Couplet 8بیت ۸

شیرها در دست روباهان اسیر

In foxes' grasp, the lion finds himself bound,

مردم بی درد بر جانان امیر

While people stand aloof, as the noble suffers around. A world inverted, where the cunning lead the brave, And the masses unmoved, to the plight of the grave.

Couplet 9بیت ۹

دوست دارم یا بسازم این جهان

I yearn to either reshape this world's way,

یا کنم در عالمی دیگر مکان

Or seek my abode in a realm far away. To mend what's here, or in new lands to dwell, In either quest, my spirit seeks to excel.

Session 4 · 5 coupletsSanctuary Doves(31)
جلسه چهارمکبوتر حرم
Couplet 1بیت ۱

ما و خیال یار و دل خود نشسته ایم

In our heart's abode, where joy is measured.

وین خانه از غبار غم و غصه شسته‌ایم

This abode, now clean of sorrow's dusty grief, A haven of solace, in love's sweet relief.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

رام کسی نبود دلم وی عجب کنون

My heart once wild, unbound, and full of yearning.

بر گیسوان یار چه آرام بسته‌ایم

Now in beloved's tresses, in peace it's turning. With gentle bonds, we've tied it soft and fair, In the locks of love, it rests with tender care.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

ضایع نبود هرچه به هجرش کشیده‌ایم

Not in vain, our trials in her absence borne,

جان از تعلق همه عالم گسسته‌ایم

For in her parting, from worldly ties we're torn. Our soul's detachment, from earthly bonds set free, In her leaving, we found our true liberty.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

یارب بهشت، دیدن خوبان عالم است

O Lord! in the world's beauty, paradise we find,

در انتظار دیدن رویش نشسته‌ایم

In gazing upon her face, in hope we sit and wait, In her visage's view, our bliss to culminate.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

همپای عشق و همسفر شور و عاطفه

Beside love, a comrade of fervor so bright,

همچون کبوتر حرم احرام بسته‌ایم

Like sanctuary doves, in our pilgrim's garb dight. Together in journey, in love's sacred rite.

Session 4 · 3 coupletsFleeting Rain(32)
جلسه چهارملحظه‌های بارانی
Couplet 1بیت ۱

داستان غم تو باد صبا میداند

Your tale of woe, the morn's breeze alone does ken,

هر که در حلقه فتد قدر هوی میداند

Its whispers hold the secrets of your sorrow then. Who in love's snare does fall, their heart can tell, The truest worth of passion in that deep well.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

سبب خنده‌ی شیرین تو ای شیرین کار

O sweet doer, the cause of your smile so dear,

سوز ما بود که دلدار روا می داند

Was the fire in our soul, to the beloved clear. That gentle burn, by your beloved seen right, A permissible flame, in her knowing sight.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

میل جاوید تو یک لحظه‌ی بارانی بود

Your lasting wish is but a fleeting rain,

چشم بارانی ام آن را به خدا میداند

These eyes of tears, by God, does ascertain. A moment's shower, your deep desire so keen, In weeping gaze, this truth is ever seen.

Session 5 · 6 coupletsThe Night without You(54)
جلسه پنجمشب بی تو
Couplet 1بیت ۱

منم آن هزار فریاد شکسته در صدایی

I am those thousand shatters in a single cry,

همه تار و پودم از عشق همه حال من هوایی

That crimson hush from Nineveh, thereby. In one voice, a multitude of screams do lie, A silence red, from ancient grounds, I signify.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

چو تو با غریبگانی، من و این همه غریبی

When you're with strangers, in their midst I stand, As all this foreignness, at your command.

وگر آشنا پسندی ز ازل تو آشنایی

Yet if you seek comfort in what's well-known, From time immemorial, it's you I have shown.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

ز تو یادگاری من غم و حس مشترک بود

In recalling you, sorrow and feeling we share,

ز من عشق و دل سپردن ز تو ناز و دلربایی

My part, devotion; yours, charm so fair. From my side, affection deep and true, From yours, enchantment in every hue.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

شب بی تو بودنم را همه با تو صبح کردم

Those evenings spent alone, in dreams I changed, To dawns with you, though in reality estranged.

به خیال بگذرانم همه عمر در جدایی

In thought, my life in separation I do wend, Through imagination, with you I spend.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

نه شکایتی نمودم که به جور می فزایی

No word of grievance from my lips did flee, Yet you, in response, increased your cruelty.

نه وفا طلب نمودم که تو تیغ می گشایی

No plea for faithfulness did I declare, But you, your sword of betrayal did bare.

Couplet 6بیت ۶

تو چو مریم مقدس چه لطیف و دلنشینی

As gentle and pure as the holy Mary so dear,

به لباس مهرورزی و نشان پارسایی

In the robe of love and godliness, you appear. Your sweetness mirrors that of Mary, Clad in love and virtue and dignity.

Session 5 · 5 coupletsWave of Sorrow(51)
جلسه پنجمموج غم
Couplet 1بیت ۱

همچو مجنون شهره در دیوانگی

In madness's grip, as Majnun once was caught,

می کشد آخر مرا ویرانگی

To a similar fate, my own ruin is brought. In insanity deep, as legend's lover was, My ruin's course by devastation's jaws.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

از غمت گفتم به دریا موج شد

I told the sea of my sorrow, and it turned to wave,

کوفت سر بر ساحل بیگانگی

Striking its brow on the shore, so grave. Against the strand of estrangement, it did beat, Echoing my loneliness in every retreat.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

شمع جان را سوختم در کوی تو

In your lane, the candle of my soul I burned,

یافتم بال و پر پروانگی

And in its flame, a moth's wings I discerned. Upon your path, my essence I did spend, Finding in the fire, a moth's fate to tend.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

قصه ی سوز جگر ناگفتنی است

The tale of the heart's blaze is beyond speech,

همچنان چشم تو در فتانگی

As are your eyes in their guile, each. Inflamed in passion, yet mute in its cry, Like the wily look in your own eye.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

با که گویم قصه‌ی این غصه را

Whom shall I find, my sorrow to share,

تا نیابم شیوه‌ی فرزانگی

Without crossing the line of sage care? In sharing this burden, heavy and grim, How to stay the course of wisdom's hymn?

Session 6 · 4 coupletsThis love(23)
جلسه ششماین عشق
Couplet 1بیت ۱

عاقبت این عشق بنیانم شکست

This love, it rent my foundation asunder,

رشته تدبیر من از هم گسست

Shattered the threads of plans, cast them yonder. In its wake, my schemes are no more, Leaving me adrift on a strange, new shore.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

آنچه در افسانه خواندیم و سمر

In legends old and tales we oft peruse,

عشق آمد با حیات من ببست

Love came to bind itself, not to refuse. To my life's thread, its fate it did choose, In woven tales, its presence it imbues.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

هر که گفت افسانه است این عشق کاش

Who claims love's but a fable, void and bleak,

عاشقی می دید و با او می نشست

I wish they'd sit with lovers who do seek, To share their heart, their truth, not mild or meek, In such company, the true love they'd speak.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

ناله های سوزناکش می شنید

So they might hear the sad cries that soar,

فاش میدید آنچه می گویند هست

And witness love's truth knocking at the door. Clearly see that what's spoken is no lore, But a deep existence, felt to its very core.

Session 6 · 5 coupletsDance of Stars(27)
جلسه ششمرقص ستاره
Couplet 1بیت ۱

آن شب که آسمان همه رقص ستاره بود

That night, when stars in heaven twirled, wondrously lit,

گویی مرا تولد ماهی دوباره بود

A fresh lunar light, upon me did sit. I seed a new moon for me, in sky unfuried.

Couplet 2بیت ۲

احساس می وزید و دل از جای می رمید

Emotion surged, and heart did trace, To where its home, its rightful space,

نقش حقیقی که چنان استعاره بود

A vision of truth, metaphorical, In image form, profound, allegorical.

Couplet 3بیت ۳

دستی پر از محبت و چشمی پر از نگاه

A hand brimmed with tender love's glow, An eye, with gaze that pierces so,

بر آن جمال کار من و دل نظاره بود

Upon such beauty, my work and heart attend, In watchful awe, where admiration and art blend.

Couplet 4بیت ۴

پنهان روانه می شد و آرام می گذشت

It moved in secret, silent in its glide, O'er sleepers resting, flowing like the tide,

بر خفتگان هر آنچه نمی ماند چاره بود

What lingered then, in quiet's deep conclusion, Was but the answer, in dream's sweet illusion.

Couplet 5بیت ۵

می ماند دل به حسرت و آه و دریغ و درد

The heart, in longing, sighed with deep regret, With pain, it dwelled where sorrows met,

در عمق سینه هم جگری پاره پاره بود

In the chest's depths, another heart lay riven, Torn apart, by grief's harsh edicts given.
